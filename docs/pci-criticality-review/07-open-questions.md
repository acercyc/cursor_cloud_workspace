# Open questions and candidate experiments

The plan asked for a list of gaps. The load-bearing one is still true after 2024–2025 work.

## Causal gap (highest priority)

**No study has causally tuned a well-defined distance-to-criticality while measuring Casali/Comolatti PCI in the same system.**

Maschke *predicts* PCI from spontaneous metrics. D’Andola *modulates* bistability and sPCI in slices. Shew *tunes* E–I and evoked entropy in culture. Deco-line models *tune* Hopf parameters and compute sfPCI on BOLD. These are the scaffolding, not the experiment.

**Candidate A — slice / culture with a branching-ratio clamp.** Combine D’Andola’s sPCI pipeline with a Wilting-style m̂ estimate under titration of excitation, inhibition, and neuromodulators. Pre-register: sPCI is an inverted-U in m̂, peaking near 1, falling for both m̂ ≪ 1 and m̂ > 1. Include a bistability readout (OFF-period probability) to see whether m̂ or OFF-rate is the better mediator.

**Candidate B — TMS–EEG plus criticality in the same session, beyond three anesthetics.** Repeat Maschke with (i) NREM vs REM in the same night, (ii) sub-anaesthetic vs anaesthetic ketamine, (iii) a classic psychedelic. Dual outcome: PCI and a pre-registered avalanche + chaos + PCF battery. This fills the psychedelic cell of the quadrant.

**Candidate C — animal closed loop.** In rodent or NHP, optogenetic or pharmacological gain control with online m̂, intermittent TMS- or electrical-perturbation complexity. Ask whether PCI-like complexity follows m̂ when arousal is held constant (pupil, EMG).

**Candidate D — DoC test of Maschke’s clinical hope.** Resting-state criticality vs PCImax vs CRS-R vs covert-command fMRI in the same patients. The falsifier: high PCI / low global criticality in a focally preserved island, or the reverse.

## Conceptual / measurement gaps

1. **Signed vs unsigned distance.** Does PCI fall on *both* sides of avalanche criticality in one preparation, or only on the subcritical/bistable side? Xenon was supposed to be the supercritical human case and was not, on avalanche metrics.
2. **Which transition is necessary?** A within-dataset horse race: avalanche DCC, LLE, PCF, Solovey-style linear stability, Stikvoort-style irreversibility, OFF-period rate — all as competitors to predict PCI. Maschke started this; PCF already lost at sensor level.
3. **Frequency support.** Toker/Maschke chaos tests live on slow rhythms; ketamine and some DoC EEGs lack those peaks. Is edge-of-chaos “consciousness support” an artifact of having a 1–6 Hz oscillator?
4. **Spatial grain.** Can a scalar criticality index ever match PCI’s site map in lesioned brains? If not, the clinical replacement story is over.
5. **Nonequilibrium vs criticality.** Stikvoort 2025 explains sfPCI by broken detailed balance. Are irreversibility and branching-ratio near-criticality redundant descriptions of the same regime, or can they be dissociated (e.g. a detailed-balance critical network vs a strongly driven subcritical one)?
6. **Power-law hygiene.** Apply Touboul–Destexhe / Beggs–Timme standards (finite-size collapse, surrogate batteries) to the Maschke pipeline before treating DCC as a clinical biomarker.
7. **Psychedelic PCI.** Still essentially missing. Until it exists, “near-critical = high PCI = conscious” cannot be tested on the high-entropy flank.
8. **Seizure PCI** (opportunistic, ethically constrained): stereo-EEG perturbation complexity around ictal recruitment, with supercriticality assays. Tests the right-hand low-PCI quadrant.
9. **Arousal matching.** Hold behavioral arousal constant (or measure it continuously) while moving criticality, to kill the remaining arousal confound.
10. **Model fidelity.** Rebuild Deco/Hopf experiments with a TMS-like transient (not a sustained bifurcation punch) and an EEG-forward model, then compute *Casali* PCI, not BOLD PCIst. If the inverted-U survives, the in silico existence proof gets serious.

## What would change the synthesis

- **Toward identity (“yes, PCI *is* distance”):** Candidate A or C shows a clean inverted-U; psychedelic PCI rises with Toker-style proximity; DoC scalars match PCImax.
- **Toward “no, only bistability”:** OFF-period rate fully mediates PCI; m̂ and LLE add nothing; Hopf-critical networks with intact Up states still score low PCI.
- **Toward coincidence:** independent manipulations move PCI and criticality in opposite directions after OFF-periods are controlled.

Present evidence is not there yet. The contingent reading in the [synthesis](06-synthesis.md) is the one that survives the current table.
