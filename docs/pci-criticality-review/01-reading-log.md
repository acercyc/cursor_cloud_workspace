# Reading log

Format: citation + link · one-line claim · bearing on the guiding question · depth.

## Deep-read (central)

1. **O’Byrne J, Jerbi K (2022).** How critical is brain criticality? *Trends in Neurosciences* 45:820–837. [doi:10.1016/j.tins.2022.08.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tins.2022.08.007)  
   *Claim:* Avalanche and edge-of-chaos criticality are the transitions most relevant to brain function; **near-criticality** (not exact criticality) is the more plausible operating regime; distance-to-criticality is a useful state metric.  
   *Bearing:* Supplies the taxonomy of non-equivalent criticalities and the “near, not exact” stance used throughout.  
   *Depth:* Deep on highlights/abstract/structure; body paywalled — arguments cross-checked against Zimmern 2020 and Maschke 2024.

2. **Zimmern V (2020).** Why brain criticality is clinically relevant: a scoping review. *Front. Neural Circuits* 14:54. [doi:10.3389/fncir.2020.00054](https://doi.org/10.3389/fncir.2020.00054)  
   *Claim:* Human criticality markers (avalanches, LRTCs, branching) change in sleep, anesthesia, epilepsy, development, and psychiatry; clinical translation is real but methodologically messy.  
   *Bearing:* Maps which empirical markers belong to which clinical states; warns that “criticality” in the clinic is a family of assays, not a single number.  
   *Depth:* Deep (open review).

3. **Beggs JM, Plenz D (2003).** Neuronal avalanches in neocortical circuits. *J. Neurosci.* 23:11167–11177. [doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.23-35-11167.2003](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.23-35-11167.2003)  
   *Claim:* Cortical networks in vitro show avalanche size exponent ≈ −3/2 and branching parameter σ ≈ 1, i.e. a critical branching process that can maximize information transmission without runaway excitation.  
   *Bearing:* Defines avalanche/absorbing-state criticality and the branching-ratio marker that later work (and PCI-as-perturbation) implicitly invokes.  
   *Depth:* Deep.

4. **Kinouchi O, Copelli M (2006).** Optimal dynamical range of excitable networks at criticality. *Nat. Phys.* 2:348–351. [doi:10.1038/nphys289](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys289)  
   *Claim:* Dynamic range of an excitable network is maximized at the critical point of a non-equilibrium phase transition.  
   *Bearing:* Why a perturbation (TMS) should produce the richest, most graded responses near criticality — the computational reason PCI *could* index criticality.  
   *Depth:* Deep.

5. **Wilting J, Priesemann V (2018).** Inferring collective dynamical states from widely unobserved systems. *Nat. Commun.* 9:2325. [doi:10.1038/s41467-018-04725-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-04725-4)  
   *Claim:* Naive branching-ratio estimates under-subsample; a consistent multi-step estimator finds in vivo cortex in a **reverberating** regime (m̂ ≈ 0.98, not 0 and not 1).  
   *Bearing:* Strongest empirical case for *near*-critical (slightly subcritical) operation; also a methodological landmine for anyone who treats σ = 1 as proven in scalp EEG.  
   *Depth:* Deep.

6. **Casali AG et al. (2013).** A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior. *Sci. Transl. Med.* 5:198ra105. [doi:10.1126/scitranslmed.3006294](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3006294)  
   *Claim:* PCI = Lempel–Ziv complexity of the binarized, significant TMS–EEG source matrix, normalized; high in wake/REM-like conditions, low in NREM, midazolam/xenon/propofol, and VS; independent of sensory/motor report.  
   *Bearing:* Defines the measure. Origin is IIT (integration + differentiation), not criticality theory.  
   *Depth:* Deep.

7. **Casarotto S et al. (2016).** Stratification of unresponsive patients by an independently validated index of brain complexity. *Ann. Neurol.* 80:718–729. [doi:10.1002/ana.24779](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.24779)  
   *Claim:* Benchmark ROC on report vs no-report yields **PCI\* = 0.31** (PCImax) with 100% Se/Sp in the benchmark; 94.7% of MCS above cutoff; 9/43 VS/UWS above cutoff (possible covert consciousness).  
   *Bearing:* Clinical gold-standard for “high vs low PCI”; ketamine/dreaming sit on the conscious side of the cutoff despite unresponsiveness.  
   *Depth:* Deep.

8. **Comolatti R et al. (2019).** A fast and general method to empirically estimate the complexity of brain responses to transcranial and intracranial stimulations. *Brain Stimul.* 12:1280–1289. [doi:10.1016/j.brs.2019.05.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brs.2019.05.013)  
   *Claim:* PCIst (dimensionality reduction + state-transition count) matches PCI accuracy on TMS–EEG and generalizes to SPES/SEEG; computable in <1 s.  
   *Bearing:* Shows the construct is not an artifact of one Lempel–Ziv pipeline; still perturbational, still not a spontaneous criticality assay.  
   *Depth:* Deep (preprint + publisher abstract).

9. **Pigorini A et al. (2015).** Bistability breaks-off deterministic responses to intracortical stimulation during non-REM sleep. *NeuroImage* 112:105–113. [doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.02.056](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.02.056)  
   *Claim:* During N3, a stimulus triggers a slow wave + OFF-period (high-frequency power collapse); after the down-state, activity resumes but **phase-locking to the input is gone**.  
   *Bearing:* Mechanism of low PCI: not “the network is dead” but **bistability aborts causal chains**. Criticality lens: down-states are a departure from the asynchronous near-critical regime.  
   *Depth:* Deep on argument; methods from abstract + later reviews.

10. **Rosanova M et al. (2018).** Sleep-like cortical OFF-periods disrupt causality and complexity in the brain of unresponsive wakefulness syndrome patients. *Nat. Commun.* 9:4427. [doi:10.1038/s41467-018-06871-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06871-1)  
    *Claim:* Pathological OFF-periods occur in awake UWS cortex (never in healthy wake); they locally break causality and prevent global PCI build-up.  
    *Bearing:* Same mechanism in DoC as in NREM; strongest mechanistic account of low PCI, and only *indirectly* a criticality account.  
    *Depth:* Deep.

11. **Solovey G et al. (2015).** Loss of consciousness is associated with stabilization of cortical activity. *J. Neurosci.* 35:10866–10877. [doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4895-14.2015](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4895-14.2015)  
    *Claim:* In monkey ECoG, anesthesia (propofol or ketamine–medetomidine) reduces the density of eigenmodes near the instability boundary; recovery reverses this. Stability is a network-level, correlational phenomenon.  
    *Bearing:* Direct “distance from dynamical criticality / edge of instability” result for LOC. **Tension:** later chaos-test papers report *increased* chaoticity under GABAergic anesthesia — different operationalization of “stability.”  
    *Depth:* Deep.

12. **Toker D et al. (2022).** Consciousness is supported by near-critical slow cortical electrodynamics. *PNAS* 119:e2024455119. [doi:10.1073/pnas.2024455119](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2024455119)  
    *Claim:* Modified 0–1 chaos test on slow cortical oscillations: wake sits near edge-of-chaos; anesthesia and generalized seizures move away; psychedelics may move *closer*; proximity may index consciousness in DoC.  
    *Bearing:* Most-cited *direct* empirical link between a specific criticality (edge of chaos of slow rhythms) and conscious state; **does not measure PCI**. Information-richness (LZ-like) co-varies but is a spontaneous, not perturbational, measure.  
    *Depth:* Deep (PMC).

13. **Maschke C, O’Byrne J, et al. (2024).** Critical dynamics in spontaneous EEG predict anesthetic-induced loss of consciousness and perturbational complexity. *Commun. Biol.* 7. [doi:10.1038/s42003-024-06613-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-024-06613-8)  
    *Claim:* On the Sarasso 2015 propofol/xenon/ketamine dataset, unconsciousness (propofol, xenon) moves *away from avalanche criticality* (subcritical) and *further into chaos*; ketamine (disconnected consciousness) stays near wake. Resting-state criticality metrics predict individual PCImax with ~6.5% LOSO error.  
    *Bearing:* **The single most direct empirical answer to the guiding question.** Also shows the two criticality types can move in *opposite* directions under the same drug. n = 15; healthy anesthesia only.  
    *Depth:* Deep (full paper).

14. **Sarasso S et al. (2015).** Consciousness and complexity during unresponsiveness induced by propofol, xenon, and ketamine. *Curr. Biol.* 25:3099–3105. [doi:10.1016/j.cub.2015.10.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2015.10.014)  
    *Claim:* Propofol: local, low PCI (mean ~0.24). Xenon: global stereotypical slow wave, even lower PCI (mean ~0.17). Ketamine: wake-like PCI (mean ~0.44) plus vivid dream reports.  
    *Bearing:* Dissociates unresponsiveness from PCI; gives the two distinct routes to *low* PCI (failed integration vs failed differentiation) that Maschke later maps onto criticality.  
    *Depth:* Deep.

15. **D’Andola M et al. (2017).** Bistability, causality, and complexity in cortical networks: an in vitro perturbational study. *Cereb. Cortex*. [doi:10.1093/cercor/bhx122](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhx122)  
    *Claim:* Slice PCI (sPCI) rises when neuromodulation (NE + carbachol) reduces Up/Down bistability; excitability boosts help less.  
    *Bearing:* Causal-ish microscale: **reducing bistability raises perturbational complexity** without a whole brain or IIT measurement. Closest interventional evidence short of tuning a branching ratio while measuring PCI.  
    *Depth:* Deep.

## Targeted skims (bridge, models, tensions, confounds)

16. **Kim H, Lee U (2019).** Criticality as a determinant of integrated information Φ in human brain networks. *Entropy* 21:981. [doi:10.3390/e21100981](https://doi.org/10.3390/e21100981)  
    *Claim:* In a 78-node Stuart–Landau connectome model, Φ and pair-correlation (edge-of-synchrony) peak together at criticality; propofol sedation reduces both.  
    *Bearing:* Modeling link from criticality to an IIT-adjacent quantity, not PCI itself.

17. **Kim H, Lee U (2021).** Criticality creates a functional platform for network transitions between internal and external processing modes. *Front. Syst. Neurosci.* 15:657809. [doi:10.3389/fnsys.2021.657809](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2021.657809)  
    *Claim:* Near-critical synchronization fluctuations create windows for switching internal vs external modes; lost under propofol / UWS.  
    *Bearing:* Why near-criticality would support the *capacity* PCI tries to probe (susceptibility + integration).

18. **Sanz Perl Y, Escrichs A, Tagliazucchi E, Kringelbach ML, Deco G (2022).** Strength-dependent perturbation of whole-brain model working in different regimes. *PLoS Comput. Biol.* 18:e1010662. [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010662](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010662)  
    *Claim:* Hopf whole-brain models in a **subcritical fluctuation** regime (not the oscillatory/supercritical regime) show richer PCI-like responses to sustained perturbation.  
    *Bearing:* In silico PCI is regime-dependent; “criticality” here is Hopf bifurcation distance, not avalanches. Adapted PCI on BOLD is not TMS–EEG PCI.

19. **Stikvoort W et al. (2025).** Nonequilibrium brain dynamics elicited as the origin of perturbative complexity. *PLoS Comput. Biol.* 21:e1013150. [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013150](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013150)  
    *Claim:* Simulated fMRI PCI (sfPCI, PCIst-style) tracks nonequilibrium / time-irreversibility / asymmetric effective connectivity across wake, sleep, anesthesia, DoC.  
    *Bearing:* Alternative “origin story” for PCI: **broken detailed balance**, not (only) avalanche criticality. Complementary, not identical.

20. **Schartner MM et al. (2017).** Increased spontaneous MEG signal diversity for psychoactive doses of ketamine, LSD and psilocybin. *Sci. Rep.* 7:46421. [doi:10.1038/srep46421](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep46421)  
    *Claim:* LZc / ACE / SCE of spontaneous MEG rise under psychedelics vs wake.  
    *Bearing:* Spontaneous diversity can exceed wake; PCI was not measured. Tension for “PCI = distance to criticality” if psychedelics are *closer* to criticality (Toker) but PCI is already near ceiling.

21. **Farnes N et al. (2020).** Increased signal diversity/complexity of spontaneous EEG, but not evoked EEG responses, in ketamine-induced psychedelic state. *PLoS One* 15:e0242056. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0242056](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242056)  
    *Claim:* Sub-anaesthetic ketamine: spontaneous LZc/ACE/SCE ↑; **PCI unchanged** vs wake.  
    *Bearing:* Clean dissociation: PCI ≈ *capacity* (level); spontaneous LZ ≈ *content* richness. PCI does not track every move in complexity/criticality space.

22. **Massimini M et al. (2010).** Cortical reactivity and effective connectivity during REM sleep in humans. *Cogn. Neurosci.* 1:176–183. [doi:10.1080/17588921003731578](https://doi.org/10.1080/17588921003731578)  
    *Claim:* TMS responses in REM recover wake-like differentiation despite paralysis.  
    *Bearing:* High PCI / complex TEPs in REM; criticality not measured.

23. **Massimini M et al. (2005).** Breakdown of cortical effective connectivity during sleep. *Science* 309:2228–2232. [doi:10.1126/science.1117256](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1117256)  
    *Claim:* TMS during early NREM yields a local, simple response (the PCI precursor).  
    *Bearing:* Historical rationale for perturbing cortex rather than using sensory stimuli.

24. **Touboul J, Destexhe A (2017).** Power-law statistics and universal scaling in the absence of criticality. *Phys. Rev. E* 95:012413. [doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.012413)  
    *Claim:* Non-critical models can exhibit avalanche power laws and scaling hallmarks used in the experimental literature.  
    *Bearing:* Methodological confound: “we saw a power law” ≠ “we were at criticality.”

25. **Beggs JM, Timme N (2012).** Being critical of criticality in the brain. *Front. Physiol.* 3:163. [doi:10.3389/fphys.2012.00163](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2012.00163)  
    *Claim:* Checklist of what would actually demonstrate criticality (finite-size scaling, exponent relations, etc.).  
    *Bearing:* Standard for not over-claiming EEG avalanche analyses (including Maschke’s truncated power laws).

26. **Sinitsyn DO et al. (2020).** Detecting the potential for consciousness in unresponsive patients using the perturbational complexity index. *Brain Sci.* 10:917. [doi:10.3390/brainsci10120917](https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci10120917)  
    *Claim:* Independent DoC sample (n = 24): PCImax > PCI\* in 11/12 MCS (~92% sensitivity); 4/11 UWS (36%) also high.  
    *Bearing:* PCI’s clinical track record is not a one-dataset result.

27. **Shew WL, Plenz D (2013).** The functional benefits of criticality in the cortex. *Neuroscientist* 19:88–100. (and Shew et al. *J Neurosci* 2009/2011 on evoked pattern entropy maximized at E–I balance.)  
    *Claim:* Stimulus-evoked pattern diversity peaks at avalanche criticality in cultures.  
    *Bearing:* Direct microscale ancestor of “PCI should peak at criticality,” cited by Maschke as refs 23–24.

28. **Colombo MA et al. (2019).** The spectral exponent of the resting EEG indexes the presence of consciousness during unresponsiveness induced by propofol, xenon, and ketamine. *NeuroImage* 189:631–644. [doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.01.024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.01.024)  
    *Claim:* Aperiodic slope steepens in propofol/xenon unconsciousness, not ketamine; spectral exponent correlates with PCI.  
    *Bearing:* A “criticality-related” spectral marker that co-travels with PCI in the same dataset.

29. **Carhart-Harris RL, Friston KJ (2019).** REBUS and the anarchic brain. *Pharmacol. Rev.* 71:316–344. [doi:10.1124/pr.118.017160](https://doi.org/10.1124/pr.118.017160)  
    *Claim:* Psychedelics flatten the energy landscape / increase entropy; related but not identical to criticality.  
    *Bearing:* Conceptual frame for “above-wake complexity” without TMS–PCI data.

30. **Sarasso S, D’Ambrosio S, Fecchio M, et al. / reviews 2021–2024** on sleep-like dynamics after injury (e.g. **Nat Commun 2024** “Sleep-like cortical dynamics during wakefulness…” [doi:10.1038/s41467-024-51586-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51586-1)).  
    *Claim:* Local sleep-like OFF-periods in injured awake cortex explain network failure.  
    *Bearing:* Updates Rosanova 2018; keeps the bistability mechanism current.

## Papers looked for and not treated as primary

- No study was found that **causally tunes** a branching ratio or Lyapunov spectrum **in the same human (or animal) and measures TMS–EEG PCI**. Closest: D’Andola sPCI + neuromodulation; Shew evoked entropy + E–I drugs in culture; whole-brain Hopf models with *simulated* PCI.
- Kim & Lee ~2019–2020 do **Φ / PCF**, not PCI. Deco/Kringelbach tradition does **simulated** PCI on BOLD/Hopf, not Casali PCI.
