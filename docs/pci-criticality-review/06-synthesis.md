# Phase 4 — Synthesis

## Essay: is PCI an operational measure of distance from criticality?

**Answer: partly — a correlated capacity readout, not a calibrated distance.**

The attractive identity is easy to state. Critical systems maximize susceptibility and the diversity of responses to a kick (Kinouchi & Copelli 2006; Shew & Plenz). PCI is literally the algorithmic complexity of a cortical kick. Therefore PCI *should* peak at criticality and fall off on both sides: subcritical die-out (propofol-like local TEPs) and supercritical stereotype (a global slow wave, or a seizure). Casali et al. (2013) built PCI from IIT, but IIT’s integration–differentiation balance is computationally the same knife-edge that criticality theory sells as a phase transition. On that view, PCI *is* distance-from-criticality, in different units.

The evidence supports a **weaker, still interesting** claim.

**What is established.** In healthy humans, PCI is high in states that, when measured, also look near-critical or wake-like on spontaneous assays, and low in states that look away from avalanche criticality: wake and anaesthetic ketamine versus propofol and xenon (Sarasso 2015; Maschke et al. 2024). Maschke is the only study that takes Casali PCI and a serious criticality battery in the same people. Resting EEG dynamics predict PCImax to a few hundredths on a 0–1 scale in that sample. Independently, the failure mode of PCI is now mechanistically specific: **OFF-periods / bistability** abort causal movies (Pigorini 2015; Rosanova 2018; D’Andola 2017). That failure mode is what a subcritical or absorbing branching process *is*, at the level of narrative. Shew-style culture work already showed evoked pattern entropy peaking at E–I criticality. So the correlation is not a coincidence of one EEG fashion. It is the expected geometry of a network that must both spread and not stereotype a perturbation.

**What is assumed if one says “PCI measures criticality.”** First, that “criticality” names one axis. It does not (O’Byrne & Jerbi 2022). Maschke’s unconscious subjects move **subcritical** on avalanches and **more chaotic** on Lyapunov/0–1 statistics at the same time. Solovey (2015) would have you say they become **more stable**. Toker (2022) would have you say they leave the edge of chaos — without telling you in which direction until you pick a test. Edge-of-synchrony (PCF) did not even co-move with PCI in Maschke. A single “distance” would have to say which transition, at which frequency, with which estimator.

Second, that the relationship is **monotone through the psychedelic and seizure flanks**. Sub-anaesthetic ketamine raises spontaneous diversity and leaves PCI flat (Farnes 2020). That is what you expect if PCI is a **pass/fail on capacity** with a wake ceiling, not a potentiometer on entropy. Psychedelic PCI is mostly unmeasured; Toker’s LSD result (closer to edge of chaos) would be a PCI prediction only under the identity view. Seizures should be far from the useful critical region with **low** differentiation; collapsing “distance” without a sign would put seizures and deep anesthesia at the same place for different reasons.

Third, that predicting PCI from resting criticality in **intact, pharmacologically modulated** brains licenses the same in **lesioned** brains. Maschke warns it does not. PCI’s clinical value is spatial and causal: which cortex still answers. A global branching ratio cannot replace that map.

**What dissociates.** (1) Spontaneous Lempel–Ziv vs PCI (Farnes). (2) TEP amplitude vs PCI (xenon). (3) Avalanche vs chaos vs synchrony vs linear stability vs nonequilibrium (Maschke vs Solovey vs Kim & Lee vs Stikvoort 2025). (4) Unresponsiveness vs PCI (ketamine, REM, LIS). (5) IIT rhetoric vs criticality rhetoric: both can generate PCI-like statistics; the TMS experiment does not adjudicate metaphysics.

**Necessity, contingency, coincidence?** Not coincidence — too many independent methods point at the same computational bottleneck. Not necessity in the identity sense — PCI can stay high while other complexity/criticality needles keep moving, and low PCI is explained more tightly by **bistability** than by a measured exponent. **Contingent:** in intact cortex, staying out of the bistable, subcritical-absorbing regime is *how* the system sits near the branching/Hopf/chaos edges that make a kick complex. PCI indexes that **functional regime**. Calling the regime “near-critical” is often fair, if you specify the transition. Calling PCI a yardstick of distance-to-criticality is more than the causal evidence can carry.

Until someone **tunes** m or a Hopf parameter (optogenetics, pharmacology with a verified branching assay, or a well-constrained model with a *TMS-like* pulse) and watches Casali PCI move, the operational claim should stay correlative.

## Three-bucket summary

**Established**

- PCI/PCIst discriminate report vs no-report in sleep and several anesthetics and stratify DoC (Casali 2013; Casarotto 2016; Comolatti 2019).
- Low PCI has a network mechanism: stimulus-triggered OFF-periods (Pigorini; Rosanova; D’Andola).
- Human/animal cortex often occupies a *near*-critical reverberating regime, not a proven exact critical point (Wilting & Priesemann; O’Byrne & Jerbi).
- In one pharmacological dataset, spontaneous avalanche and chaos metrics predict PCI (Maschke 2024).
- Spontaneous diversity and PCI are not the same variable (Farnes 2020).

**Assumed (widely, sometimes usefully)**

- Integration + differentiation (IIT) and criticality are two names for one operating point.
- EEG power laws index that point (Touboul & Destexhe say otherwise).
- Distance to criticality is one-dimensional and unsigned.
- Resting-state criticality can replace TMS in DoC (Maschke’s healthy-anesthesia prediction does not license this).

**Dissociates**

- Ketamine: unresponsive, high PCI, near-wake criticality (consciousness ≠ responsiveness).
- Xenon: high-amplitude stereotyped TEP, low PCI, avalanche-subcritical (amplitude ≠ criticality ≠ integration).
- Sub-anaesthetic ketamine / psychedelics: spontaneous LZ up, PCI not (content ≠ capacity).
- Avalanche vs edge of chaos vs edge of synchrony vs linear stability vs irreversibility (different arrows under the same drug).
- Intact vs lesioned cortex (local PCI islands vs global criticality scalars).

## How to use this as a researcher

Treat PCI as a **perturbational capacity** assay. Treat criticality metrics as **hypotheses about why** capacity is high. When they agree, you have a mechanism sketch. When they disagree, believe the TEP and the OFF-period, then ask which criticality assay was the wrong projection.
